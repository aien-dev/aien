# Allen: mission and endpoint

Drake's direction, 2026-10-20 (programme issue, formerly aien-architecture-mv#278):

Build the first personal Allen from Drake's project-development conversations with Claude Code, Codex and other agents. Learn his intended outcomes, constraints, corrections and what actually worked. Preserve his language, including profanity; avoid converting style or frustration into unsupported claims about feelings, permission or correctness.

Allen's mission is to develop a continuing, correctable understanding of his human and provide useful anticipation while preserving the human's ability to change their mind. The endpoint is one persistent identity across the iMac, Raspberry Pi, Spark and MacBook: purpose, memory, authority and evidence remain coherent through interaction changes, disconnection, restart and model replacement. This programme tests operational continuity and usefulness.

## The one demonstration every phase serves

Drake expresses a bounded project goal; Allen explains its interpretation, performs admitted work, provides evidence, accepts a correction, survives restart and handles a related task with less human effort. P6 extends the same demonstration across all four devices; P7 tests learning and anticipation over time.

## Names

- **Allen P0 to P7**: delivery phases (sequence, not priority). Distinct from the earlier numbered phases in aien-architecture-mv#269.
- **Allen B0**: the first qualified personal-assistance release, after P5 passes its gates. Its model, memory, runtime and dataset identities are recorded separately. A model checkpoint is not Allen's identity.
- **ALLEN** (ADR 0035): the durable subject bound to one LogicalAgentId, holding standing intent, held knowledge references and the identity-to-memory binding. This repository does not redefine it.

## Hard completion gate

The programme is not complete at B0. It closes only when P0 to P7 are each closed with acceptance evidence and every mandatory external prerequisite listed in `docs/PHASES.md` is closed with its own evidence. "100% complete" means every acceptance item in the frozen, reviewed scope has candidate-bound passing evidence, no unresolved blocker, and independent review. It does not mean omniscient knowledge of a person or a proof of consciousness; Allen's continuing mission stays open-ended.
